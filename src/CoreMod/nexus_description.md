[size=4][i]In-Game Menu, FPS-Drop Fix & Mod Manager for Gold Mining Simulator[/i][/size]

[hr]

[size=5][b]What Does This Mod Do?[/b][/size]

Ever get annoyed having to quit your game, open config text files in Notepad, and restart just to change a simple mod setting? Or noticed that [i]Gold Mining Simulator[/i] starts lagging and losing FPS the longer you play?

[b]Milex GMS1 CoreMod[/b] fixes that. It gives you a clean in-game menu accessed anytime by pressing [font=Courier New]Insert[/font], stops the game from slowing down over long sessions, and manages all your mods right inside the game.

[quote][b]Important:[/b] CoreMod is the required foundation for all Milex mods (such as [i]Production Tuner[/i] and [i]Claim Monitor[/i]). Install this first![/quote]

[hr]

[size=5][b]Highlights[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b]Change Mod Settings Live In-Game ([font=Courier New]Insert[/font]):[/b] Press [font=Courier New]Insert[/font] at any time to open the menu. Sliders, checkboxes, and search bar: tweak speeds, capacities, and HUDs live while playing. No restarts needed. You can drag the menu anywhere on screen and adjust UI scaling so it looks great on 1080p, 1440p, or 4K.
[*] [b]Stops Long-Session FPS Drops:[/b] Automatically cleans up accumulated memory leaks in the background when you fast travel, save your game, or open your laptop. Keeps your game running smooth and stutter-free even during 5-hour mining marathons. Got a sudden lag spike? Click [b]"Clean Memory Now"[/b] in the menu to instantly clear clutter.
[*] [b]Turn Mods On or Off with One Click:[/b] Don't want a specific mod active right now? Just uncheck it in the sidebar. It turns off instantly and returns your game to 100% normal vanilla without quitting.
[*] [b]Play in Your Own Language:[/b] Works out of the box in English and German. Want the mod in Spanish, French, Polish, or another language? You can generate translation files with one click in-game!
[/list]

[size=5][b]Quick Install Guide[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*] Make sure you have [b]BepInEx 5 (x64)[/b] installed in your game folder.
[*] Drop [font=Courier New]Milex_GMS1_CoreMod.dll[/font] into your [font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\BepInEx\plugins\[/font] folder.
[*] Start the game, load your claim, and press [font=Courier New]Insert[/font]!
[/list]

[hr]

[size=5][b]Controls[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b][font=Courier New]Insert[/font]:[/b] Open / Close the mod menu (camera movement is locked while open so you can click freely).
[*] [b]Click & Drag Header:[/b] Move the menu window anywhere on your screen.
[*] [b]UI Scale Slider:[/b] Make the menu bigger or smaller to fit your monitor resolution.
[*] [b]Clean Memory Now Button:[/b] Instantly clears memory clutter and displays how many MB of RAM were freed.
[/list]
[i](Prefer a different key? You can rebind [font=Courier New]Insert[/font] to any key in the menu or config file).[/i]

[hr]

[size=5][b]Settings & Configuration[/b][/size]

You can change everything directly in the in-game menu ([font=Courier New]Insert[/font]). If you prefer editing files, your config is saved at:
[font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\BepInEx\config\Milex_GMS1_CoreMod.cfg[/font]

[list]
[*] [font=Courier New]MenuToggleKey[/font]: Change the hotkey from [font=Courier New]Insert[/font] to whatever key you like.
[*] [font=Courier New]PauseGameOnMenu[/font]: Choose whether opening the mod menu freezes the game world or lets it keep running in the background (Default: [font=Courier New]false[/font]).
[*] [font=Courier New]UIScale[/font]: Adjust how large the menu looks on your screen (Default: [font=Courier New]1.0[/font]).
[*] [font=Courier New]EnableMemoryCleaner[/font]: Turn automatic background memory cleaning on or off (Default: [font=Courier New]true[/font]).
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation & Requirements[/b][/size]

[b]Requirements:[/b]
[list]
[*] [i]Gold Mining Simulator[/i] on Steam.
[*] [url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases]BepInEx 5 (version 5.4.21 or newer, x64)[/url] – the standard mod loader.
[/list]

[b]Step-by-Step Installation:[/b]
[list=1]
[*] If you don't have BepInEx yet: Download [b]BepInEx_x64_5.4.x.zip[/b], open it, and drag the contents into your main game folder [font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\[/font] (where [font=Courier New]GoldRushTheGame.exe[/font] is).
[*] Download this mod.
[*] Put [font=Courier New]Milex_GMS1_CoreMod.dll[/font] into your [font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\BepInEx\plugins\[/font] folder.
[*] Launch the game!
[/list]

[b]Uninstallation:[/b]
Delete [font=Courier New]Milex_GMS1_CoreMod.dll[/font] from your [font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\BepInEx\plugins\[/font] folder. Your game and save files remain 100% untouched.

[size=5][b]Want to Help Translate the Mod?[/b][/size]

CoreMod makes it super easy to translate all Milex mods into your language:

[list=1]
[*] Press [font=Courier New]Insert[/font] in-game and click [b]General[/b] in the sidebar.
[*] Turn off [b]"Use Game Language"[/b] and select your language in the dropdown (French, Polish, Spanish, etc.).
[*] Click [b]"Create Templates"[/b] when prompted.
[*] The mod will instantly create editable translation files in:
[font=Courier New]\Gold Rush The Game\BepInEx\plugins\Milex GMS1 Mod Localization\[/font]
[*] Open the files with Notepad, translate the text, and save!
[/list]

[quote][b]Did you translate it?[/b]
Post your translated file in the [b]Comments / Posts[/b] section here on NexusMods! We will gladly include it in the next update so other players speaking your language can use it too![/quote]
